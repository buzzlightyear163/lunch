Anatomy of a sandwich

One block. Three trades.

  1. 01

    You hit buy.

    1.00 SOL into a pump.fun coin with slippage at 15%. The quote says 21.46M tokens. Your transaction waits for a spot in the next block.

  2. 02

    A bot gets there first.

    It sees your buy and lands 2.00 SOL of the same coin one position ahead of you. The price jumps 10.2%, still inside your 15%.

  3. 03

    You fill at its price.

    Your 1.00 SOL now buys 19.49M tokens, 9.2% fewer than quoted. It is inside your slippage, so the trade goes through.

  4. 04

    It sells straight into you.

    One position later the bot dumps everything and keeps 0.094 SOL. That came out of your trade.

Diagnose

Lunch Check

Every swap you made on pump.fun or PumpSwap, checked against the transactions around it in the same block. Each hit links to the three transactions on Solscan, so you can verify it yourself.

RPC endpoint

LUNCH reads public Solana RPC by default. It is free and rate-limited, so scans take a while. Paste your own endpoint (Helius, QuickNode, Triton) to make every tool faster. It is stored in this browser only.

Fix

Slippage Tuner

Slippage is how far you let the price move before your trade fails. Set it high and a bot has room to push the price up to that limit. The tuner reads the coin's latest trades and finds the tightest setting that still lands.

Method

How LUNCH finds a sandwich.

No guessing and no private data. Everything comes from public Solana transactions and is computed in your browser.

Step 1

Pull your swaps

LUNCH reads your latest transactions and keeps the ones that bought or sold a token on pump.fun's bonding curve or PumpSwap.

Step 2

Read the block

For each swap it opens the block it landed in and looks at the transactions just before and just after yours on the same pool.

Step 3

Match the bot, price the damage

One wallet buying before you and selling after you is a sandwich. LUNCH then replays your trade on the pool's reserves from before the bot moved them, and the gap is what you lost.

Every result shows the front-run, your trade and the back-run as Solscan links. If a number looks wrong, the transactions are one click away.
